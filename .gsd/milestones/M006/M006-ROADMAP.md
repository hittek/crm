# M006: CRM ↔ Chatbot Bridge — Roadmap

## Slices

- [x] **S01: Activity Logging from Chatbot** `risk:high` `depends:[]`
  > After this: every chatbot conversation is asynchronously logged as an Activity on the matched Contact in CRM — channel, duration, message count, and outcome (resolved/escalated) captured

- [x] **S02: Lead Auto-creation** `risk:high` `depends:[S01]`
  > After this: when a conversation includes a name + phone/email not matching any existing Contact, a new Contact and Deal (in Lead stage) are automatically created in the org's CRM

- [x] **S03: Chatbot Quote → CRM Sync** `risk:medium` `depends:[S01]`
  > After this: quotes generated by the chatbot appear as Quote records in CRM linked to the Contact/Deal within 5 seconds; duplicate detection prevents double-posting on webhook retry
